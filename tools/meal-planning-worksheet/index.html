How to use this meal-planning worksheet

Choose a plan label and first date. Add up to 21 unique main-meal slots across seven days. Each meal needs a label, 1–24 whole servings and 0–600 whole prep minutes. Missing slots remain unplanned; they are not treated as skipped meals, completed work or a recommendation about eating frequency.

Check your chosen recipes and preferences separately. Enter 0–50 ingredient lines, each with a name, unit, total quantity needed and usable stock. Supported units are g, kg, mL, L and each. Quantities are nonnegative up to 1,000,000 with at most three decimals. Convert quantities yourself before using them together; the tool does not treat 1 kg as 1 g or combine entries across units.

Shopping formula and original fictional example

Additional quantity to buy = max(0, total needed − usable stock), calculated for each ingredient/unit. The planner uses thousandths internally to avoid common decimal subtraction artifacts. It does not add unlike ingredient quantities into a misleading grand total.

The sample starts November 16, 2026 and ends November 22. Three first-day meals each have four servings and prep times of 10, 20 and 30 minutes: 3 of 21 slots planned, 18 unplanned, 12 serving-occasions and 60 summed prep minutes. That does not mean twelve different people or sixty minutes on an automatically scheduled kitchen calendar.

Rice needed 600 g less 250 g usable stock leaves 350 g to buy. Tomatoes needed 800 g with 900 g stock leave zero to buy. Two entered units of beans less one in stock leave one to buy. Those are three independent manual ingredient lines, not automatically generated recipes or a complete ingredient list for the example meals.

Review and save deliberately

Review the report before shopping. Every edit clears the old report and disables CSV/print until reviewed again. Loading the sample, importing a validated same-tool backup and clearing the page require confirmation. Import does not merge plans. JSON keeps editable input fields; CSV includes the reviewed meal schedule, ingredient quantities, summary and limitations. Browser printing opens your device's print dialog; inspect pagination.

The separate two-page US Letter landscape PDF is a static blank/example writing sheet, not fillable and not an editable backup. Without JavaScript, the guide and static PDF remain available. Keep important backups in a place you control.

Practical questions

Does changing servings scale groceries? No. Update ingredient quantities from your chosen recipe yourself.

Can I use two units for the same ingredient? They stay separate and trigger a review note; no conversion or consolidation occurs. Combine each ingredient/unit's need and stock once to avoid duplicate counting.

Does this validate a diet, allergens or food handling? No. It organizes labels and quantities, without nutrition, allergy or food-safety checks, cost estimates, ordering or delivery.

Use the time-blocking worksheet for actual calendar windows and the weekly planner guide for broader commitments. These are separate manual tools; entries do not sync between them.
