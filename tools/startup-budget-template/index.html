Free Startup Budget Template: Costs, Reserve and Funding Gap

By NexalyPlanner · Published November 18, 2026

Back to Free Planners

Separate startup spending from retained cash, model a fixed early-trading horizon and calculate a funding gap. Includes an original Excel template and browser worksheet.

This is a bounded startup funding worksheet, not a full business plan, valuation, profit forecast or dated runway model. Monthly receipts and payments stay constant for 1–24 entered trading months. It assumes upfront funding arrives before spending and does not model within-month payment order, ramp-up, seasonality, financing fees or loan repayment unless already included in the supported cash assumptions. Model pre-opening delays separately. Positive monthly cash generation is not used to reduce the upfront funding target in this deliberately conservative model.

Enter the record and period

Enter every field, including intentional zeros. USD only; no currency conversion. Dates label the historical period, or the preparation window for the startup assumptions. The startup model’s separate month count controls its trading horizon.

How the calculation works

Upfront uses = equipment + setup + refundable deposits + entered contingency. Operating shortfall = max(0, monthly payments − monthly receipts) × months. Funding target = upfront uses + operating shortfall + retained reserve. Surplus/(gap) = entered funding − target. End cash = funding − upfront uses + (monthly receipts − monthly payments) × months. End cash can be negative; it does not test daily solvency. Deposits and equipment are cash uses here, not automatically profit expenses. Replace each assumption with source evidence and use the dated forecast for timing.

Original filled example

The fictional startup lists $10,000 equipment, $2,000 setup, $1,000 deposits and $1,000 contingency: $14,000 upfront cash uses. Monthly payments of $3,000 less receipts of $1,000 leave a $2,000 monthly cash need. Three months require $6,000. Add a $4,000 retained closing reserve to reach a $24,000 funding target. Available funding of $23,000 leaves a $1,000 gap and $3,000 modeled end cash. The $4,000 reserve is retained cash, not an extra expense or supplier payment.

Review before using the result

Check dates, missing accounts, classification, duplicate transactions and source evidence. A zero reconciliation difference or positive result is not an audit or approval to spend. This educational resource does not select accounting methods, calculate tax or certify GAAP/IFRS compliance. If the supported categories do not fit, use a reviewed accounting report rather than forcing the figures into this model.

Practical questions

Why is a blank amount rejected? Missing is different from intentional zero. Enter each supported amount and basis before calculating.
