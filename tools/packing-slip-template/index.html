How to fill out the packing slip

  1. Save your own copy. Keep the clean template and give the working file a recognizable order-and-slip filename.
  2. Enter references and addresses. Preserve the order ID, choose a unique slip ID and use the actual receiving location.
  3. List the parcel contents. One row identifies one order line or separately traceable item. Enter all four quantity inputs, including zeroes where none apply.
  4. Review the open quantity. Resolve Complete row or Check quantities in column J. Keep calculated columns I:J intact.
  5. Check the actual parcel. Match SKU, variant, unit and count to the order. Record the packer and checker under your own process.
  6. Archive and update records. Keep each issued slip; enter actual dispatch once in your order and stock records. Printing does not dispatch goods.

Filled example: ship 12 filters and keep eight open

Example Workshop has customer order ORD-1023 for 20 replacement filters and four mounting clips. Slip PS-1023-A sends twelve filters and all four clips. The filter balance remains eight; the clip balance is zero. Do not total different items to decide whether a specific line is complete.

The last row is an alternative to the second row, not a third shipment. The formula is open after = ordered − prior shipped − cancelled − this shipment. Ordered stays twenty. Changing it to twelve after the first dispatch hides the remaining commitment.

For shipment two, make a new slip and retain the first. Enter twelve in prior shipped, rather than replacing the original slip with a cumulative twenty-unit parcel. In the customer order tracker, shipped units are cumulative: update twelve to twenty for the no-cancellation case, or to seventeen with three cancelled. The two files do not synchronize.

What the quantity warnings mean

An unused row stays blank. A started row needs line ID, SKU, description, unit and four numeric quantities. Ordered must be positive; prior shipped, cancelled and this shipment must be nonnegative. Their sum cannot exceed ordered. For example, 20 ordered, 12 prior and 9 now is invalid; the workbook leaves open after blank and asks you to check quantities.

Decimal quantities are allowed for a unit such as meters, but whole physical items should use whole counts. A blank input is not an intentional zero. The form does not check duplicate IDs, address accuracy, stock availability or whether a cancellation was approved. Review those against your source order before issue.

For stock handling, follow the sales and inventory event example. If twelve units are still on the packing bench at a stock count, use the count cutoff and movement log so reserved stock is not mistaken for missing stock.

Save, print and check the working copy

Keep the workbook in .xlsx format to preserve formulas and formatting. On the required slip tab, select A2:I25, choose Print Selection, landscape orientation and fit to one page. Column J contains internal input reviews and stays outside the recipient-facing selection. Preview the output before sending; application and printer settings can change layout.

The PDF is already two US Letter landscape pages: print page one for the blank form or page two to read the fictional example. It is not a fillable PDF. For A4 paper or a phone spreadsheet app, check the print preview and adjust the paper size before printing. After importing the workbook into another spreadsheet app, test 20 ordered and 12 now, confirm eight open, save and reopen the same copy before using real orders.

For longer descriptions or more than eight lines, use clearly linked continuation slips and review the complete shipment manually. This file does not automatically create extra pages. Back up issued copies separately from the editable master.

Frequently asked questions

Is the packing slip template free?

Yes. The Excel workbook and PDF are direct downloads without an email signup or purchase. The site's paid planners are separate products.

Can I use it in Google Sheets or Word?

The offered editable file is Excel .xlsx. You can try importing a copy into another spreadsheet app and verify its calculations and layout. No native Google Sheets copy button or Word version is provided.

Does a return increase the open shipment quantity?

Not automatically. This form records ordered, dispatched and cancelled quantities. Record returned goods, condition, refunds and any replacement commitment separately under the original order; do not subtract returns from historical dispatches to disguise the event.

Does an open quantity of zero prove delivery?

No. It means the entered quantities account for the modeled commitment. Keep carrier and delivery evidence separately and review any dispute using the original records.
