What this free tool does

  • Common quantity and currency keep quotes comparable.
  • Unit prices, freight and fixed fees remain visible beside landed totals.
  • Your 0–100 scores and two weights produce an explained comparison.
  • Up to 100 editable entries, explicit device saving, JSON backup, CSV and browser PDF printing.

How to use it

  1. Name the item or specification and enter the quantity you need. Choose one currency shared by every quote.
  2. Add suppliers with their unit price, freight, fixed fees and quoted lead time. Check whether a quote covers the same specification and delivery destination.
  3. Score price and delivery from 0 to 100, with higher meaning better in both columns. Enter price and delivery weights; they can total any positive number.
  4. Calculate and compare costs and scores. Review exclusions, quality, payment terms and supplier reliability separately before making a purchase decision.

How the calculation works

Landed quote = quantity × unit price + freight + fixed fees. Weighted score = (price score × price weight + delivery score × delivery weight) ÷ total weight. Lead days and costs are displayed, but they are not converted automatically into scores. Zero total weight blocks calculation.

Worked example with fictional data

For a fictional order of 100 units, Supplier A quotes USD 2 per unit, USD 25 freight and USD 5 fixed fees: landed cost is USD 230. Price score 80 at weight 60 and delivery score 50 at weight 40 gives (80 × 60 + 50 × 40) / 100 = 68. Supplier B has score 78 in the example, but that only reflects the entered assessments.

Assumptions and common mistakes

This supplier quote comparison worksheet does not verify vendors, select the cheapest suitable product or fetch quotes. Every quote must use the same quantity, specification and currency. Tax, duties, variable freight and exchange rates are not inferred. If one supplier uses a minimum order or tiered price, request a quote for your common quantity before entering it.

Choose score definitions before seeing the final ranking so the comparison stays consistent. A delivery score is your judgment of the quoted lead time, not a prediction of punctual delivery. Include each fee once. Equal scores show all tied suppliers; the tool does not invent a tie-breaker.

Save, restore and export

Add entry creates another record. Edit any field directly or use Remove entry to delete a row. Changing inputs clears the previous report so it cannot be mistaken for current results. Choose Calculate / review results again when your changes are ready.

Save on this device keeps an editable worksheet in this browser only. On a later visit, choose Restore device save and confirm replacement. Saving drafts is allowed, but a report requires valid, complete inputs. Download backup creates a versioned JSON file for the same tool. Import backup shows the worksheet label and row count before replacing current work; it does not change the device save until you press Save.

Download CSV exports the settings, summary, row details and calculation notes. CSV is for spreadsheet review; it cannot restore this worksheet. Print / Save PDF recalculates the current entries and opens your browser’s print dialog. Choose Save as PDF where supported. The report excludes navigation, guides and product CTAs. Clear data removes the current worksheet and its device save after confirmation; keep a backup first if needed.

Frequently asked questions

Are lower prices converted into higher scores automatically?

No. You enter both scores yourself on the stated 0–100 scale. Dollars and lead days are never mixed directly into the weighted score.

Do the two weights have to total 100?

No. They must be nonnegative and total more than zero. A 60:40 split and a 3:2 split express the same relative weights.

Can quotes use different quantities or currencies?

This worksheet uses one required quantity and currency. Obtain comparable quotes or convert amounts separately using a documented rate before entering them.

Is this tool free, and does it sync between devices?

All worksheet, saving and export features are free without an account. There is no cloud synchronization. Transfer a JSON backup to another device and import it into this same tool.
