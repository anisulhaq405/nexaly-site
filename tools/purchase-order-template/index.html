How to fill out a purchase order in five steps

  1. Save a working copy. Give it a unique PO reference and enter a fixed issue date. Do not use a changing TODAY formula for an archived order.
  2. Identify both parties. Fill buyer, supplier, contact, ship-to address, requested delivery date and one currency.
  3. Add items. Enter SKU where relevant, description, positive quantity, unit and a nonnegative unit price. Keep the gray formula cells intact.
  4. Review costs. Enter tax, shipping and discount as nonnegative amounts in that currency. Fix incomplete or invalid rows before relying on the total.
  5. Approve and keep a copy. Record the approver and date under your own process, confirm supplier terms, then save the issued version. Use a separate tracker for receipts.

A filled $78 purchase order example

Example Workshop orders 20 replacement filters at $3.50 each. The line amount is 20 × $3.50 = $70. Add $8 shipping, $0 tax and $0 discount: the grand total is $78. Zero tax is a fictional input, not a rule for US purchases.

Blank item rows stay blank. A partly completed row asks you to complete it. Invalid quantity or price blocks that row; incomplete rows and invalid adjustments prevent the grand total from appearing as a finished amount.

Check units and order changes before issuing the PO

The quantity and unit price must describe the same buying unit. If a supplier quotes $3.50 per filter and you need 20 filters, enter quantity 20, unit each and unit price 3.50. If a supplier quotes a box of ten for $35, two boxes also cost $70, but the quantity is 2 and the unit price is 35. Do not enter 20 at $35 unless you intend to order twenty boxes.

Read the total only after reviewing every line

The filled workbook's $78 example is $70 merchandise plus $8 shipping, with entered tax and discount both zero. For another order, confirm the actual freight, tax and discount amounts before approval. The template does not look up tax rates or convert currencies. Keep one currency throughout the form.

Blank rows remain unused. If you see Complete row, Check quantity / price, Review rows or Check adjustments, inspect the input and review cells before treating the order as finished. Do not type over a calculated line amount to force a desired total. Save a clean original so you can recover the formulas.

Keep the issued order separate from later receipts

Suppose 12 of the 20 filters arrive first. The issued PO still records what was ordered; record the first receipt and remaining eight in the purchase-order tracker. Do not change the order quantity to 12 merely to match that delivery. Document an agreed cancellation or revised quantity separately using your purchasing process, and keep the original issued copy.

Use a fixed issue date and a unique reference, such as your own purchasing sequence. If you revise a draft before issue, save the reviewed version deliberately. The files do not maintain revision history, send orders to suppliers or record acceptance.

Excel cell guide: where to enter and review amounts

These references apply to both the Blank PO and Filled example tabs in the actual download. Work on a saved copy. Amber input cells hold your entries; gray cells contain formulas.

CellsPurposeWhat to check
A6, D6, F6PO reference, fixed issue date and currency.Use your own unique sequence. Currency labels amounts; it does not convert them.
A16:E25SKU, description, quantity, unit and unit price for ten lines.Description, numeric positive quantity and numeric nonnegative price are needed for a calculated line. Enter the buying unit explicitly even though its presence is not enforced by the formula.
F16:F25 / G16:G25Calculated line amounts / row review messages.Each valid line is rounded to two decimals. Leave unused rows fully blank.
F27 / F28:F30Subtotal / entered tax amount, shipping and discount.Enter all three adjustments as numbers, using 0 when appropriate. Tax is an amount, not a percentage.
F31Grand total.Subtotal + tax + shipping − discount, once line and adjustment checks pass.
A34 / D34Approver and approval date.Complete these under your own approval process; a numeric total does not establish authorization.

Example: B16 = Replacement filters, C16 = 20, D16 = each and E16 = 3.50 gives F16 = 70.00. With F28 = 0, F29 = 8 and F30 = 0, F31 is 78.00. Changing only F30 to a confirmed $5 discount gives 73.00. Do not enter 5% in an amount cell to represent $5.

Why is the purchase order total blank or showing a warning?

What you seeLikely causeFix before issuing
Blank line amount or totalNo valid numeric lines yet, or that row is not ready.Check the intended item row and column G. Completely blank unused rows are normal.
Complete rowAn item row is partly filled, its description is missing, or quantity/price is blank or text.Complete the description and numeric inputs, or clear all A:E entries in the unused row.
Check quantity / priceQuantity is zero or negative, or unit price is negative.Enter a positive quantity and nonnegative numeric price. An intentional zero price is allowed; a missing price is not zero.
Review rowsAt least one valid line exists alongside an incomplete or invalid line.Resolve every non-ready row in G16:G25. The visible subtotal can include valid lines while the order is still unfinished.
Check adjustmentsTax, shipping or discount is blank/text/negative, or discount exceeds subtotal + tax + shipping.Enter three nonnegative numeric amounts and confirm the discount with the supplier. Do not overwrite F31.

For the $78 example, a discount of 79 triggers Check adjustments; a discount of 78 produces a zero total. A zero total is mathematically possible and still needs a business explanation. The row checks do not validate addresses, dates, units, approvals or supplier agreement, so review those separately.

Save, print and send the reviewed purchase order

  1. Save the editable workbook under your unique PO reference. Keep a separate copy of the issued version.
  2. On the required PO tab, select A2:F34, choose Print Selection and fit it to one US Letter landscape page. Column G contains internal row checks and is outside this supplier-facing selection.
  3. Preview the exported page: supplier, ship-to, dates, units, terms, approval and total must be readable. Avoid exporting the Filled example or Instructions tab by mistake.
  4. Send the reviewed file using your own supplier communication process. Request confirmation of the reference, quantities, buying units, prices and delivery date; retain that response with the issued copy.

The provided PDF already contains two US Letter landscape pages. Print page 1 for a blank order; page 2 is a fictional completed example. A4 and native Excel mobile printing have not been certified.

Frequently asked questions

Is this purchase order template free?

Yes. Both downloads work without an email signup or purchase.

Does it calculate tax or send an order?

Enter the actual tax amount yourself. The files do not email suppliers, take payments, update stock or determine the legal effect of an order.

Can I use this in Google Sheets or Word?

The download is an .xlsx workbook, not a native Sheets template or Word document. Conversion requires checking the formulas and print layout yourself. Use the supplied PDF when you need a ready-to-print blank form.

Is the PDF fillable on screen?

No. It is a printable blank form plus a fictional example. Use Excel for editable calculations; do not expect PDF form fields or automatic totals.

Can I add more than ten Excel line items?

This version has ten rows. Splitting a large order across additional copies needs clear references and a manual total review. Do not insert rows without checking every affected formula.
