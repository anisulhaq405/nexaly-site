How to fill out a purchase order in five steps

  1. Save a working copy. Give it a unique PO reference and enter a fixed issue date. Do not use a changing TODAY formula for an archived order.
  2. Identify both parties. Fill buyer, supplier, contact, ship-to address, requested delivery date and one currency.
  3. Add items. Enter SKU where relevant, description, positive quantity, unit and a nonnegative unit price. Keep the gray formula cells intact.
  4. Review costs. Enter tax, shipping and discount as nonnegative amounts in that currency. Fix incomplete or invalid rows before relying on the total.
  5. Approve and keep a copy. Record the approver and date under your own process, confirm supplier terms, then save the issued version. Use a separate tracker for receipts.

A filled $78 purchase order example

Example Workshop orders 20 replacement filters at $3.50 each. The line amount is 20 × $3.50 = $70. Add $8 shipping, $0 tax and $0 discount: the grand total is $78. Zero tax is a fictional input, not a rule for US purchases.

Blank item rows stay blank. A partly completed row asks you to complete it. Invalid quantity or price blocks that row; incomplete rows and invalid adjustments prevent the grand total from appearing as a finished amount.

Check units and order changes before issuing the PO

The quantity and unit price must describe the same buying unit. If a supplier quotes $3.50 per filter and you need 20 filters, enter quantity 20, unit each and unit price 3.50. If a supplier quotes a box of ten for $35, two boxes also cost $70, but the quantity is 2 and the unit price is 35. Do not enter 20 at $35 unless you intend to order twenty boxes.

Read the total only after reviewing every line

The filled workbook's $78 example is $70 merchandise plus $8 shipping, with entered tax and discount both zero. For another order, confirm the actual freight, tax and discount amounts before approval. The template does not look up tax rates or convert currencies. Keep one currency throughout the form.

Blank rows remain unused. If you see Complete row, Check quantity / price, Review rows or Check adjustments, inspect the input and review cells before treating the order as finished. Do not type over a calculated line amount to force a desired total. Save a clean original so you can recover the formulas.

Keep the issued order separate from later receipts

Suppose 12 of the 20 filters arrive first. The issued PO still records what was ordered; record the first receipt and remaining eight in the purchase-order tracker. Do not change the order quantity to 12 merely to match that delivery. Document an agreed cancellation or revised quantity separately using your purchasing process, and keep the original issued copy.

Use a fixed issue date and a unique reference, such as your own purchasing sequence. If you revise a draft before issue, save the reviewed version deliberately. The files do not maintain revision history, send orders to suppliers or record acceptance.

Purchase order form, tracker or invoice?

This form records one order to a supplier. The purchase order tracker follows multiple orders, expected dates and receipts. An invoice records the supplier's request for payment. Keep the PO reference in related records so you can match the order, receipt and invoice.

Before issuing an order, use the supplier quote comparison to check pack quantities, freight and lead times. For a larger inventory workflow, see the optional Inventory & Procurement Planner.

Save and print

Save the workbook with a unique filename before editing, and keep a copy of each issued order. The PDF is ready for US Letter landscape. For Excel printing, select A2:F34 on the required PO tab, choose Print Selection and fit to one landscape page. Column G contains your review checks and stays outside the supplier-facing print selection. A4 and native Excel mobile printing have not been certified.

Frequently asked questions

Is this purchase order template free?

Yes. Both downloads work without an email signup or purchase.

Does it calculate tax or send an order?

Enter the actual tax amount yourself. The files do not email suppliers, take payments, update stock or determine the legal effect of an order.

Can I add more than ten Excel line items?

This version has ten rows. Splitting a large order across additional copies needs clear references and a manual total review. Do not insert rows without checking every affected formula.
