How to use this proposal template

Start with the client objective. Define deliverables, revision limits, acceptance criteria, excluded work, delivery timing and client dependencies. Record what happens when inputs or approvals are late. Enter “none” when a required section truly has no item; do not present an unknown obligation as settled.

A proposal supports one client engagement. A business plan describes the whole operation. This draft records neither agreement nor payment. Confirm appropriate commercial and legal terms for the actual work before relying on an offer.

Price and deposit formulas

Each line is whole quantity multiplied by USD unit price. Total adds the lines in integer cents. Proposed deposit equals total times the entered whole percentage, rounded to the nearest cent. Balance equals total minus deposit. No taxes, discounts, currency conversion or recurring billing are calculated. Confirm required additions separately before issuing a final price.

Original fictional example

One discovery workshop at $300, six page drafts at $150 each and one revision round at $200 total $1,400. A 30% deposit is $420, leaving $980. Design, coding and ongoing changes are excluded. Timing depends on client inputs and must be converted into confirmed dates before agreement. These are invented figures, not recommended market rates.

Review, export and restore

Any edit invalidates the old report and disables text export and print until reviewed again. Text export is a static draft. JSON is the editable same-tool backup. Import checks format, limits, dates and amounts and asks before replacing current entries. The separate PDF is a static two-page US Letter writing template, not fillable. Browser Print / Save PDF prints the reviewed report; inspect pagination and device options.

Limits and next step

Use 1–20 price lines, whole quantities 1–1,000 and unit prices $0–$1,000,000 with at most two decimals. Valid-until cannot precede issue date. No e-signatures, sending, payment records, CRM sync or automatic saving are provided. Assign the next review with the sales handoff guide; check hours against the consulting capacity example.

Frequently asked questions
