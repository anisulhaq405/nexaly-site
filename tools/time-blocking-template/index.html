Create a weekly time-blocking plan

Choose a Monday week-start date and a same-day start/end window. The same window applies to all seven days, including weekends. Add 1–60 named blocks and select the weekday and type: focus, fixed, break or buffer. Every block must have a positive duration inside the daily window. Overnight blocks are not supported. A weekday with no entries has its whole window free, which does not mean every free minute is available for work.

How this differs from the daily builder

This worksheet keeps a seven-day block template with a Monday date and block categories. The daily schedule builder handles one fixed day. The printable layout builder creates writing layouts. None of these promises calendar synchronization, automatic reminders or task-duration predictions.

Formulas and overlapping blocks

For each weekday, occupied minutes are the union of its entered intervals. Free minutes equal daily-window length minus occupied minutes. Duplicate task minutes equal summed block durations minus occupied minutes. Blocks touching at an endpoint do not overlap. With several simultaneous blocks, duplicate minutes can exceed the length of one conflict. Type totals sum each entered block duration; they can count overlapping promises more than once and must not be treated as unique occupied time.

Fictional weekly example

In a 09:00–17:00 window, Monday focus 09:00–10:30, a fixed call 10:00–11:00 and lunch 12:00–12:30 sum to 180 block minutes. Their union is 150 minutes, leaving 330 free and 30 duplicate minutes. A Tuesday 14:00–15:00 buffer occupies 60 minutes and leaves 420 free. The other five days are unplanned, each with 480 free window minutes. Weekly totals are 210 occupied, 3,150 free and 30 duplicate minutes across all seven days. Resolve Monday's conflict before committing to both tasks.

Use blocks as decisions, not proof of completion

Include preparation, travel, breaks and handoffs. Put realistic task scope inside a focus block and identify what can move if a fixed commitment changes. A reserved buffer is already occupied in this worksheet; remove or resize it before allocating the same time elsewhere. A block does not prove the task was completed, and this tool does not check staff qualifications or other people's availability.
