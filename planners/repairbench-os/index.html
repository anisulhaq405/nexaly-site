Description

RepairBench OS is an offline small engine repair shop manager that runs in one file, right in your browser. Made for independent repair shops, it keeps your whole workflow connected: an owner dashboard with live shop-health KPIs, a repair job pipeline from intake to collected across 8 job statuses, and connected records for customers, machines and jobs — so you can find any equipment record without chasing paper notes. Track parts inventory with reorder alerts that account for reserved stock, keep invoices, payments and profit together with per-job profitability, and see what needs attention with the built-in RepairBench Insider — automatic, rule-based findings on margin, backlog, cash and stock, all from your own saved data. Save, export and print branded business reports (PDF, Word, Excel) with your own shop name, logo and details. Everything runs offline and your records stay in your browser — no account, no monthly subscription. Works in 7 languages including right-to-left Arabic, with dark and light themes and your own JSON backups. A one-time purchase, instant download, USA demo data included.
