Description

NexalyPlanner Boutique Business Planner is an interactive, fillable browser app for boutique owners, handmade sellers, Etsy shops, online stores and growing side businesses. Ten connected sections bring your dashboard, business branding, vision and goals, products and pricing, inventory, orders and sales, clients, money, marketing, notes and key dates into one organized workspace. The built-in pricing calculator shows selling price and profit, while income and expense records update your profit dashboard automatically. Add your own logo and business details, choose from five color themes and use the undated planner year after year. It opens in a modern browser on phone, tablet or computer, saves as you type and keeps information privately on your device. No Adobe, GoodNotes, account or monthly subscription is required. A backup option helps you save and move your records when needed.
