Frequently asked questions

How is contribution margin calculated?

Subtract total variable cost per unit from the selling price. Multiply by planned units to estimate total contribution before fixed costs.

Why might break-even units be unavailable?

The unit estimate requires comparable units and a positive weighted contribution. Revenue-based analysis can still be available when unit labels differ.

Does the product-mix model account for all constraints?

It models the selected resource limit and the demand ceilings entered. Review any other production, inventory or staffing limits separately.
