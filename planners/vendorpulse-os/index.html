Description

VendorPulse OS is a private, offline supplier management and purchasing system built for boutiques, ecommerce businesses, makers, wholesalers and micro-manufacturers. Keep suppliers, materials, quotes and purchase orders connected in one browser app. Compare true landed cost per accepted unit — including freight, duty, handling, other charges and expected rejects — so a low unit price never hides the real cost. Track deposits, partial deliveries, backorders, rejected quantities, quality issues, supplier invoices and payments; then rank vendors with evidence-based scorecards for on-time delivery, fill rate, quality and cost stability. The Financial Position view connects commitments, payables, inventory value, quality loss and cash pressure. Ask the built-in Insider Expert natural-language questions about any record, calculation or workflow and receive evidence, confidence and a recommended next action. Download professional reports as branded PDF, Word, Excel or CSV files. Start with USA demo data, choose from 162 currencies and 7 interface languages, switch light or dark mode, and back up everything to JSON. Your records stay on your device.
