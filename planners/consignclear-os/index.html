Description

ConsignClear OS is an offline consignment inventory and settlement manager for resale boutiques, vintage stores, antique shops, galleries and maker collectives. Keep consignor contacts, ownership terms, commission agreements and item records together so you can see who owns each piece, what rules apply and what happens after a sale.

At intake, record an item’s SKU, condition and return-by date. Follow its status through available, reserved, sold and returned. When a sale is recorded, review the commission split alongside channel and payment-processing fees. Record refund deductions separately from sales, and use a return record for unsold stock. Record an actual payout with its payment reference after the owner has reviewed the amount; paid history is locked in the interface.

The dashboard compares payout liability with a cash estimate you enter manually and highlights aging stock. Reports bring together financial position and operating records; CSV export and JSON backup help move or protect your data. A fictional Austin, Texas sample workspace is available from Settings for exploring the workflow before entering your own records.

The downloadable app uses one offline HTML file, no login or monthly subscription, light and dark modes, navigation in seven languages and eight currency display options. Forms and reports are in English. Currency selection changes symbols and formatting only; it does not convert exchange rates. Keep JSON backups outside the browser, especially before clearing browser data or changing devices.

ConsignClear OS supports internal organization and review. It does not replace a legal consignment agreement, bookkeeping, tax or payment advice. Owners remain responsible for commission rules, refunds, taxes and payouts; software signals do not approve financial actions.
