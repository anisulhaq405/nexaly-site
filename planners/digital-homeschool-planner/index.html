Description

The Digital Homeschool Planner is a private, offline planner and record-keeper for homeschooling families — built to plan, teach, track, assess and manage your whole homeschool in one place. Manage multiple children with separate profiles, dashboards and goals; build your curriculum by subject, unit, topic and objective; schedule lessons on a daily, weekly and monthly calendar; and keep assignments and projects on track with due dates and priorities. Record grades in a clear gradebook, track attendance and learning hours, and watch progress with built-in analytics that spot strengths and weak topics early. When it is time to report, print or save professional progress reports and transcripts, or export to CSV. A built-in AI assistant guides you through features, daily priorities and study support — all offline. Everything runs in your browser and stays on your device — your data, your family, 100% private. Works in 7 languages including Arabic. A one-time purchase, instant digital download, yours forever.
