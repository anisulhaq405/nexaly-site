Description

NexalyPlanner Offline Inventory & Procurement Planner is a standalone HTML business system for small businesses, workshops, warehouses, makers, wholesalers, retailers and operations teams. Manage products and SKUs, suppliers and supplier-product links, warehouses and locations, stock movements, controlled adjustments and transfers from one local workspace. The procurement workflow connects purchase requests to purchase orders, approvals, partial receiving and traceable goods-received notes (GRNs). Low-stock review, reorder recommendations, demand planning, alerts and inventory reports help organize replenishment decisions. Invoice and receivables tracking, CSV exports and browser print-to-PDF output are included. Choose from seven interface languages, multiple currencies, light and dark themes, and add your own business name, logo, contacts and address. The optional local password is a convenience feature, while JSON backup and import tools help protect and move browser-stored records. No cloud account, server database, online verification or monthly subscription is required. Always verify important quantities, valuations, orders and reports against physical counts, supplier documents and accounting records.
