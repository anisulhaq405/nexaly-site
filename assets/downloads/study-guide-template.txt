Subject and topic:
Mathematics | Adding fractions with different denominators

Learning objective and source:
Add 3/4 and 1/8 and explain the common denominator. Source: your assigned lesson and checked course examples.

Key concepts:
Equivalent fractions represent the same value. Use a common denominator before adding numerators.

Explain it in your own words:
Rename quarters as eighths: 3/4 = 6/8. The pieces must have the same size before their counts can be added.

Worked question and checked answer:
3/4 + 1/8 = 6/8 + 1/8 = 7/8. Check: 0.75 + 0.125 = 0.875, which equals 7/8.

Recall questions without notes:
Why use eighths here? What is 1/2 + 1/4? After trying: 1/2 = 2/4, so the sum is 3/4.

Weak area and correction:
First attempt added denominators. Correction: rename both fractions, keep the common denominator and add numerators.
