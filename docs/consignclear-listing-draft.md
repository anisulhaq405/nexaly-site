**Description**

ConsignClear OS is an offline consignment inventory and settlement manager for resale boutiques, vintage stores, antique shops, galleries and maker collectives. Keep consignor contacts, ownership terms, commission agreements and item records together so you can see who owns each piece, what rules apply and what happens after a sale.

At intake, record an item's SKU, condition and return-by date. Follow its status through available, reserved, sold and pending payout. When a sale is recorded, review the commission split alongside channel and payment-processing fees. Keep customer returns and settlement adjustments visible as separate records instead of silently changing paid history. Use owner-reviewed payout batches to document what is owed and what has been paid.

The command dashboard compares payout liability with available cash and highlights aging stock. Reports bring together the shop's financial position and operating records; CSV export and JSON backup help move or protect your data. The included Austin, Texas sample workspace gives you a way to explore the workflow before entering your own records.

The app runs from one HTML file in a modern desktop browser. It requires no login or monthly subscription and stores records locally on your device. It includes light and dark modes, seven interface languages and eight currency display options. Currency selection changes the symbol and formatting only; it does not convert exchange rates. Download JSON backups regularly, especially before clearing browser storage or moving to another computer.

ConsignClear OS supports internal organization and review. It does not replace a legal consignment agreement, accounting, tax or payment advice. Owners remain responsible for commission rules, refunds, taxes and payouts. Its automated signals do not approve payments, markdowns or refunds.
