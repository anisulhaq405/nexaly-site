## Research and differentiation

Primary references were read for narrow definitions: Google Search Console reporting; Google Analytics acquisition scopes; HubSpot sales reports; Microsoft dashboard design, dashboard/report distinction, star schema, row-level security and Power Automate approvals; W3C use of color; SBA business finances. Full source links appear in articles.json and on the relevant pages. Provider docs support definitions, not fictional business outcomes. Discovery also inspected Semrush's analytics-dashboard article (https://www.semrush.com/blog/analytics-dashboard/) and Databox's SEO dashboard gallery (https://databox.com/dashboard-examples/seo). These demonstrate the broad explanation and template-gallery approaches. Search also surfaced Salesforce's dashboard samples PDF and Mammoth CRM examples; those were discovery leads, not full-document verification. No claim of fixed Google positions 1–3, localized SERP coverage, keyword volume/difficulty or competitor traffic is made.

The new guides add original small worked examples, explicit populations/denominators, source cutoffs, exceptions and an owned next action. They distinguish user/session acquisition, open/closed CRM records, dashboard/report concepts, join duplication, outcome/action units and gross/net return. All figures are explicitly fictional; no vendor performance or market benchmark is asserted. Source text is paraphrased briefly.
