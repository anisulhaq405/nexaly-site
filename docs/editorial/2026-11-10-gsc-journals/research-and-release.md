# November 10 GSC journal research and changes

## Scope and method

The November 10 GSC drilldowns contain 38 journal URLs: 31 discovered/currently not indexed and seven crawled/currently not indexed. The other affected URLs are tools, products, guides, downloads and endpoints; they are outside this editorial release. Original publication dates, URLs, titles, feature images, product prices and application behavior are retained.

Each journal received a fresh intent query and three distinct external publisher comparisons. Google US/English results could not be verified: direct Google retrieval produced a CAPTCHA and the web reader could not access the search page. The competitor order below is a research selection from available search, not Google positions 1–3. No ranking causation, backlink strength, keyword difficulty, traffic or ranking improvement is asserted.

The observations identify content features that make a page useful for the same intent. Their effect on ranking is a hypothesis; no private search-engine signals were available. Search snippets alone were not treated as a complete page review. Pages were fetched and their body/section structure inspected; ClickUp was read through the web reader after its direct request returned 403. The inaccessible Institute of Spa Management page was replaced by Mindbody. SAP's script-dependent page was replaced by Smartsheet.

## Shared findings

Business-plan competitors commonly provide fillable narratives and market/operating sections beside financial examples. The existing NexalyPlanner examples were retained and extended with specific acquisition, volume, cash, channel or capacity decisions. Planner competitors often provide format tables, previews and import guidance; this release adds bounded acceptance tests, purchase calculations and distinct roles for overlapping URLs. Operational-template pages benefit from usable definitions and reconciliations; the additions address due cohorts, mixed units, weights and record joins. No arbitrary word-count expansion or unsupported product feature was added.

## Technical validation and limits

The new sections are inside the existing article containers, contain contextual links and use original illustrative data. Existing downloadable files and tool algorithms are not changed; unit conversion and channel scenarios are manual examples, not new workbook features. Financial examples retain their stated omissions. References to Goodnotes, Apple, NIST and SBA are provider/primary-source guidance rather than invented compatibility or compliance claims.

Verification is recorded separately in the PR after source checks, numeric/date tests, responsive inspection and deployment. A content update does not prove Google indexing or a top-three ranking.
