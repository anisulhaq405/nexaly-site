## Scope and evidence

Build one focused customer-order group. Existing main guide covers basic payments, quantities and exceptions; new guides deepen distinct tasks rather than repeat the same general intent. Broad order-change results often concerned construction, so that topic was narrowed to cancellation and stock release. Search-result evidence supports the existence of these workflows, not quantified demand or verified US top-ten positions. The supplied CSV exports did not provide matching order/backorder/shipment/cancellation rows in the text check; no search volume is claimed.

Primary references reviewed: Shopify individual fulfillment and inventory states documentation; Adobe Commerce order status and reservations documentation. Their platform behavior is attributed as such. Nexaly manual examples and arithmetic are independently explained, not copied platform procedures. Free tool claims checked against the existing worksheet feature list and instructions; paid product claims checked against its published user guide. No automatic split-shipment, cancellation or synchronization capability is promised.
