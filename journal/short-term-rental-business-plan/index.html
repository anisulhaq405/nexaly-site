Short-Term Rental Business Plan: Occupancy, Turnover & Cash

Plan a short-term rental with available-night occupancy, stay-level cleaning costs, fees, cash timing and a lower-booking scenario. Includes a reconciled property example.

Example basis: Businesses, amounts, fees and outcomes in the worked examples are fictional planning assumptions in USD, prepared November 6, 2026. They are not market averages, observed customer results or income promises.

Define the property, operating rights and guest offer

A short-term rental business plan must identify the property, the right to operate it, the intended guest, delivery responsibilities and the cash needed between bookings. This example models one fictional accommodation unit. It is not a property-investment return forecast, an offer to buy real estate or evidence that a particular location permits short stays.

Specify sleeping capacity, amenities, minimum stay, check-in method, maintenance owner and guest-support coverage. Validate demand with dated local evidence and comparable offers whose location and stay conditions actually match. A large nightly rate from a peak weekend is not a monthly average, and a platform listing does not establish the right to host.

Both occupancy figures are mathematically valid, but answer different questions. Label the denominator when comparing months. Removing nights from availability can raise the reported percentage without increasing revenue. Preserve the calendar and reason for each blocked night rather than editing the denominator to improve the dashboard.

At a fictional room rate of $150, room revenue is $2,700 and room average daily rate is $2,700 / 18 = $150. Cleaning fees are separate. Do not add a cleaning charge to room revenue and still call the resulting amount room ADR. Taxes collected for another party and refundable deposits need separate treatment under the actual arrangement.

The 3% is an assumption, not a current platform quote. Confirm the actual fee base, refunds, cleaning agreement and settlement rules. The result excludes financing, depreciation, owner time, tax and other unlisted costs; it is not an investment yield or take-home income. Repair reserves should be a deliberate cash allocation rather than an unexplained percentage.

Six turnovers at 2.5 hours require 15 cleaning hours. If only 12 hours are available on the relevant dates, the plan has a three-hour gap despite empty nights elsewhere in the month. Secure actual backup capacity or change the booking pattern. A monthly total does not prove that a same-day turnaround can be delivered.

Test fewer nights and delayed collections

A lower-booking case has 12 occupied nights in four three-night stays. At the same assumptions, room revenue is $1,800 and guest cleaning charges $160. Fees are $58.80, cleaning $220, night-sensitive costs $144 and fixed costs $1,800. The modeled remainder is negative $262.80. Occupancy is 12/25 = 48%; do not reuse six cleaning charges when the number of stays changes.

In the base case, suppose only $2,400 of the $2,940 has been received by the month-end cutoff. With $5,000 opening cash and all $2,434.20 listed costs paid, closing cash is $4,965.80. Another $540 is expected later under the example assumptions. Settlement timing can therefore reduce cash while the operating bridge remains positive.

Record booking date, stay date, expected settlement, actual receipt, cancellation and refund separately. Use actual platform statements to reconcile gross amounts and deductions. Do not count a held refundable deposit as operating money or assume a future booking cannot be cancelled.

Build a launch and service checklist for the actual location

Verify local hosting permission, lease or association conditions, insurance, registration, tax and service obligations with the responsible authorities and relevant agreements. The IRS rental recordkeeping resource is a federal reference; it does not decide local permission or the tax treatment of this simplified example.

Before opening the calendar, test the access process, maintenance response, cleaning handoff and backup contact using fictional guest details. State what happens when a repair removes available nights. Keep a spending ceiling for furniture and supplies and compare the downside cash need with actual funding before committing.

Use the business-plan template, startup checklist and planning guide for the wider work. Does high occupancy prove profitability? No: rate, stay length and costs matter. Can this substitute for a lending or tax model? No; use complete property-specific records and the recipient's requirements.
