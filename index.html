Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about buying, downloading, and using Nexaly planners.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No subscription — every planner is a one-time purchase. You can preview anything for free without an account; you only sign in to access your downloads after buying.

What exactly do I get?

A digital planner (a PDF designed for apps like GoodNotes and Notability, or an interactive planner that opens in your browser). After checkout you get an instant download link — the file is yours to keep.

Which devices and apps does it work with?

Our PDF planners work with GoodNotes, Notability, and any app that opens PDFs, on iPad, tablet, or desktop. Interactive planners run in any modern browser on phone, tablet, or computer.

How do I download after buying?

You'll get a download link on the confirmation page and by email straight after payment. You can also find your files anytime under “My downloads” when signed in.

How do bundles work?

Pick a Bundle of 3, 5, or 10 and choose any planners you like at a lower price per planner. The Whole Collection unlocks every planner plus new releases for a year. Prefer just one? Buy any planner individually at its own price.

Do I get free updates?

Yes — whenever we improve a planner you own, the updated version is free. Just re-download it from your account.

Can I get a refund?

Absolutely. If a planner isn't right for you, email us within 14 days and we'll make it right. See our Refund Policy for details.

Still have a question?

We're happy to help — head to our Contact page and drop us a message.

Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We want you to love your Nexaly planner. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

14-day happiness guarantee

If you're not satisfied with a planner, contact us within 14 days of your purchase and we'll work with you to make it right — including a full refund where appropriate.

Digital products

Because our planners are digital and downloaded instantly, we review refund requests individually to keep things fair for everyone. In most cases — a planner that didn't meet expectations, a technical issue we can't resolve, or an accidental duplicate purchase — we'll happily issue a refund.

How to request a refund

Email support@nexalyplanner.com with the email address you used at checkout and the name of the planner. We'll respond within one business day.

Bundles & collections

Bundle and Whole Collection purchases are covered by the same 14-day guarantee. Refunds apply to the bundle as a whole.

Questions? Visit our Contact page — we're glad to help.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using nexalyplanner.com you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the shop: your email address (for your account, downloads, and any newsletter you opt into), your name if you provide it, and order details. Payment is handled by our payment provider — we never see or store your full card details. We also collect basic, anonymised usage data to understand how the site is used.

How we use your information

To deliver your planners and downloads, send order confirmations, provide support, send updates you've opted into, prevent fraud, and improve our products and website.

Sharing

We share information only with the trusted services that help us operate — such as our payment processor, email, and analytics providers — and only as needed. We do not sell your personal data.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the site work and optional analytics cookies to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email or by contacting us.

Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations, and we take reasonable measures to protect it.
