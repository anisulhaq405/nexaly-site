How to Use the Sales Management System

Manage leads, orders, FIFO inventory, customers, suppliers, expenses, invoices, employees, payroll and sales profit with this Nexaly guide.

Home › Product Guides › Sales Management System

The Sales Management System connects an order to inventory, cost of goods, customer balances and profit. Set up master records first so transactions flow to the correct reports.

Quick start: Create products, customers and suppliers; enter opening FIFO inventory; record sales through Orders; then verify COGS, receivables and payment status.

Set up and use the planner

1Configure business settings

Choose currency, language and business details. Decide who may use the file and how backups will be controlled.

2Create products and FIFO opening stock

Add each product and enter verified inventory cost layers. FIFO COGS depends on the chronological quantities and costs saved.

3Create customers, suppliers and leads

Use consistent names and avoid duplicate records. Convert or connect a lead only when the relationship is clear.

4Record purchases and sales orders

Enter incoming stock through the appropriate purchase workflow. For a sale, select the correct customer, product, quantity and price so inventory and COGS update together.

5Invoice and record payment

Review the order before creating the PDF invoice. Record partial or final payments against the right customer and transaction.

6Review expenses, staff and performance

Enter expenses and supported staff records consistently. Review P&L, inventory value, receivables and ROAS against source documents.

Worked example: FIFO sale and gross profit

  1. Enter 10 units at $12 cost, followed later by 10 units at $14.
  2. Record a sale of 12 units at $25 each.
  3. Verify FIFO uses 10 × $12 plus 2 × $14 for $148 COGS.
  4. Confirm sales are $300 and gross profit before other expenses is $152.
  5. Create the invoice and record the customer payment without duplicating the sale.

After the example, compare the dashboard and source records. If a total is unexpected, correct the original transaction rather than editing an unrelated summary.

Protect your offline data

Your planner records are stored locally in the browser on the device you use. They do not automatically sync to another browser or device. Create a supported backup regularly, especially before clearing browser data, moving the HTML file, changing devices or reinstalling a browser. Keep dated backup copies in a location you control and test that you can locate them.

What the product does not do

  • The system does not automatically sync with banks, stores, ad platforms or payroll authorities.
  • Financial and payroll outputs depend on entered data and are not professional accounting or legal advice.
  • Access controls and backups remain the owner’s responsibility.

Troubleshooting

COGS is unexpected

Inspect FIFO opening layers, purchase dates, quantities and the order’s product selection.

An invoice changed profit twice

Check whether the order and invoice were both entered as separate sales instead of one connected workflow.

ROAS does not match the ad platform

The system uses manually entered spend and revenue; verify attribution period and values.
