What RenewGuard OS does

RenewGuard OS is a private, offline workspace for small businesses that manage recurring vendor agreements. It helps you see what renews, when notice is due, what it costs and who must make the decision. The included Austin North Creative LLC records are fictional training data. Replace them before using the figures for your own business.

Its “Insider” analysis is local and rule-based. It does not send messages to vendors, interpret a contract for you or cancel a service.

Quick start

  1. Download and extract the buyer ZIP. Open NexalyPlanner_RenewGuard_OS.html in a modern desktop browser.
  2. Explore the fictional US records, then open Settings to set your business name, currency, language and theme.
  3. Add a vendor and enter the signed contract’s start and end dates, notice days, renewal terms, owner and billing amount.
  4. Check the calculated Notice Deadlines and Renewal Calendar. Compare them with the original agreement, including the required delivery method for cancellation.
  5. Add obligations, costs and usage reviews, then record an owner-confirmed renewal decision and target date.
  6. Export a JSON backup after setup and after material changes. Keep copies outside this browser profile.

Dates and costs

The notice date equals the entered contract end date minus the entered notice days. Annualized commitments use monthly × 12, quarterly × 4, weekly × 52 and annual × 1; a one-time cost is excluded from recurring annual spend. Scores and labels are operational indicators based on your records, not legal, credit, accounting or insurance risk ratings.

Storage and backup

RenewGuard stores records in the local browser profile on the device that opens the downloaded HTML file. It has no account or cloud sync. Clearing browser data or changing browser profiles can remove records. Save at least two secure JSON backups, make one before device migration, and test a restore in a separate profile.

Languages and display

The interface offers English, Arabic, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and French. Arabic uses a right-to-left layout. A light and dark theme toggle is available. Currency is a display setting, not a live foreign exchange converter.

Troubleshooting

  • If the app does not open, extract the ZIP first and use Open With in Chrome or Edge.
  • If records appear missing, check that you are using the same device and browser profile.
  • If a notice deadline looks wrong, verify the signed end date and advance notice period.
  • If charts are empty, add active contracts with dates and recurring costs.

Review the signed contract and confirm decisions with the responsible owner. This guide and the app are organizational aids, not legal, tax or financial advice.
