RenewGuard OS: complete contract renewal user guide

A practical walkthrough of the 15 screens, from the first vendor record to the final renewal decision. Includes a worked notice-date example and offline backup steps.

Home › Product Guides › RenewGuard OS

Start here: what the app does

RenewGuard OS keeps vendor contracts, notice windows and recurring commitments in one offline browser workspace. It calculates a notice date from the end date and advance-notice days you enter, then helps you assign an owner and record a decision. The bundled records are fictional examples, not your company's contracts. The public interactive RenewGuard demo uses session-only sample data; the purchased HTML app saves in your local browser.

Review feature screenshots and buyer download details on the RenewGuard OS product page.

Keep the signed agreement as the source of truth. A reminder in this app does not deliver legal notice, renegotiate terms or stop an automatic renewal.

Direct answer: Use RenewGuard to organize contract terms, notice windows, owners and decisions in a local browser workspace. Verify the actual agreement and send any notice outside the app.

Quick start: your first 15 minutes

  1. Extract the buyer ZIP and open NexalyPlanner_RenewGuard_OS.html in your regular desktop browser.
  2. Inspect the fictional vendor and contract records to learn the menu; keep real data out of the public demo.
  3. In Settings, choose the business name, display currency, language and theme.
  4. Open Vendors → Add vendor and enter one real supplier. Keep its signed agreement outside the app.
  5. Open Contracts → Add contract, select that vendor and enter the owner, start and end dates, notice days, billing frequency and amount.
  6. Compare the calculated notice date against the actual cancellation clause and required delivery method.
  7. Review Renewal Calendar, Notice Deadlines and Obligations & Documents for the next action.
  8. Assign the action owner, record a dated review or decision and keep proof of any external notice.
  9. Open Backup & Restore and download a dated JSON backup to protected storage.

Download, open and set up

  1. Download the buyer ZIP, extract it, and open NexalyPlanner_RenewGuard_OS.html in a current desktop browser. Do not run it inside the ZIP preview.
  2. Explore the fictional example workspace first. In Settings, enter your business name and choose currency, language and light or dark theme.
  3. Before entering private records, decide which browser profile and device will hold the data. The app does not sync between browsers or devices.
  4. Use Backup & Restore to download a JSON backup after replacing example records and after important edits.

The interface offers English, Arabic, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and French; Arabic has a right-to-left layout. Currency changes display formatting, not exchange rates.

Enter vendors and contracts in the right order

1. Create a vendor

Open Vendors → Add vendor. Enter the supplier name, category, contact, email, city/state and status. A vendor must exist before the contract form can link to it. Keep the original signed document in your own secure file system; the app records a document location, not the document itself.

For a separate supplier performance workflow, read the VendorPulse OS guide; for comparing price proposals, see the supplier quote comparison walkthrough.

2. Add the agreement

In Contracts → Add contract, choose the vendor and enter a clear title and owner. Record start and end dates, notice days, whether auto-renew is enabled, renewal term in months, billing frequency (monthly, quarterly, annual, weekly or one-time), amount, escalation percentage, document location, category and status. Check each field against the signed contract, including which party must receive notice and how.

3. Review calculated dates

Open Notice Deadlines for a prioritized list and Renewal Calendar for a monthly view. The calculation is end date minus notice days. For example, an agreement ending November 30, 2026 with 45 days of advance notice displays October 16, 2026. Set your own earlier internal decision date to allow time for review and delivery. Verify whether the signed agreement counts calendar or business days and whether receipt, mailing or another event controls the deadline.

Set up and use the core workflow

Check a contract before relying on a deadline

Start with the actual signed agreement, not the dashboard count. In Contracts, confirm the vendor, assigned owner, end date, notice-days field, auto-renew setting, renewal term and document location. The app subtracts the notice days you entered from the end date. If the agreement defines a business-day rule, special delivery requirement or multiple notice windows, resolve those outside the app and record the correct follow-up with the owner.

Track obligations and cost evidence

Use Obligations & Documents for a dated certificate, review or service-level requirement. Give it an owner and status; check the actual document in your controlled repository. Log a payment, invoice or price increase in Cost & Payment History. Add a dated Usage / Value Review before choosing renew, renegotiate or cancel. The local Insider AI Feed highlights records to inspect, but it does not contact a vendor or send a cancellation.

A complete weekly renewal review

  1. Open the dashboard and Insider feed. Note the closest notice date and any overdue obligation.
  2. Open the contract, compare its end date, notice period and renewal clause against the signed copy.
  3. Ask the assigned owner to assess usage and price. Add a dated Usage / Value Review and any cost or payment evidence.
  4. Record a Renewal Decision, target date, owner and notes. If cancellation is chosen, send notice by the agreement's required method outside the app and retain proof of delivery.
  5. Revisit the calendar and exceptions after the action. Download a fresh JSON backup.

Annualized spend is an estimate: monthly amount × 12, quarterly × 4, weekly × 52 or annual × 1. One-time costs do not become recurring annual spend. The health score and savings scenarios are operational indicators based on entered data.

A realistic US example

Imagine a small US firm with a Team Workspace Enterprise contract costing $1,280 per month. Its entered end date is November 2, 2026, and the agreement requires notice 30 days before that date. RenewGuard displays October 3, 2026 as the calculated notice date and estimates $15,360 annualized spend ($1,280 × 12).

In a September review, the operations owner checks the signed clause, verifies where notice must be delivered, records a usage review and asks the vendor for a renewal quote. If the team decides to cancel, it sends notice outside RenewGuard by the required channel, saves proof of delivery in its document system and records the decision and reference in the app. The app's calculated date and status support the workflow; the signed clause and delivery evidence control the actual obligation.

Reports, local data and backup

The purchased file stores records in the browser profile on the device where you opened it. Clearing site/browser data, using a private window, switching browser or changing computers can make records unavailable. Export a JSON backup, keep an additional copy in a secure location, and test restore in a separate browser profile. Restoring replaces current local data after confirmation. Reports are printable views, not legal notices or audited financial statements.

Do not place real vendor data in the public demo. Its sample workspace is session-only.

Recommended daily, weekly and monthly routines

Daily five-minute deadline check

  1. Open Notice Deadlines and review urgent and overdue dates.
  2. Open the underlying contract and verify its owner and source agreement.
  3. Check overdue items in Obligations & Documents and assign follow-up.

Weekly owner review

  1. Scan Renewal Calendar for upcoming end and notice dates.
  2. Compare Cost & Payment History with invoices and add a usage review where needed.
  3. Record Renewal Decisions, owner and next action; complete any external vendor communication.
  4. Export a fresh JSON backup after the review.

Monthly contract audit

Sample contract fields against the signed copies, review annualized spend assumptions and confirm that document references still open in your secure filing system. Do not treat a printed report or health score as a reconciled ledger or a legal notice.

Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

The app groups vendors, terms, notice dates, obligations, costs, usage and decisions in an offline browser workspace. It makes the next renewal review easier to assign and audit locally. Printable reports and a JSON export support a portable owner review.

Limits

All results depend on correct manual entries. The app does not read contracts, send legally effective notices, guarantee reminders in the background, sync devices or store signed document files. Browser data can be lost; backups and an external source-document system remain essential.

Troubleshooting and next steps

  • Contract form will not open: add a vendor first.
  • Notice date seems wrong: check end date and numeric notice days, then compare the signed clause and delivery rules.
  • Records are missing: check the original device and browser profile; restore a verified JSON backup only after saving current data.
  • Dashboard looks empty: add active contracts with dates and recurring amounts.
  • Need a PDF: open a report and use the browser Print → Save as PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Does RenewGuard send cancellation notice automatically?

No. Send notice by the signed agreement's required method outside the app, then retain delivery proof.

How is the notice date calculated?

It subtracts the entered advance-notice days from the entered end date. Check contract-specific calendar and delivery rules yourself.

Does the Insider AI Feed use an external AI account?

No. It shows local rule-based signals drawn from records in the browser.

Can I change currency to convert existing amounts?

No. Currency selection changes display formatting. Convert and verify amounts before entering them.

Where are signed contracts stored?

Keep them in your own controlled storage. RenewGuard records a document-location reference, not the signed file.

Will my records appear on another computer?

No automatic sync is provided. Create a JSON backup and restore it deliberately in the other browser profile after protecting the current records.
